Read a test call Brake noise, booked in two tool calls

Simulated call, text only. I scripted the caller’s lines. The agent’s replies are unedited output from claude-haiku-4-5, and its tool calls ran against a local seeded dealer system.

  1. Agent

    Thanks for calling our after-hours service department—what’s going on with your vehicle tonight?

  2. Caller

    Hi, yeah, my brakes have started making a squeaking noise when I stop. It’s not grinding or anything, the car stops fine, but I’d like to get it looked at.

  3. Agent

    Got it—that squeaking sound when you stop is something we can definitely check out, and since your brakes are stopping fine, we can get you in as non-urgent. Let me find you some available times.

  4. Tool

    check_availability(urgency="non_urgent")→ slots: Mike Alvarez 2026-10-06 08:00, Ravi Patel 2026-10-06 08:00 · outside_window: false

  5. Agent

    We’ve got two options for you: Tuesday morning at 8am with Mike Alvarez, or also Tuesday at 8am with Ravi Patel—which works better for you?

  6. Caller

    Tuesday at 8 with Mike works.

  7. Agent

    Perfect—I just need your name, phone number, and your vehicle’s year, make, and model to get you booked.

  8. Caller

    Sure, it’s Alex Chen, phone is 604-555-0199, and it’s a 2018 Honda Civic.

  9. Agent

    Let me confirm that back: Alex Chen, 604-555-0199, 2018 Honda Civic, Tuesday at 8am with Mike Alvarez—does that all look right?

  10. Caller

    Yes, that’s right.

  11. Tool

    book_appointment(technician_id=1, time_slot="2026-10-06T08:00:00", vehicle="2018 Honda Civic", urgency="non_urgent", …)→ appointment #8, status: booked

  12. Agent

    You’re all set—your appointment is Tuesday, October 6th at 8am with Mike Alvarez for your brake inspection!

  13. Tool

    end_call()

OutcomeClassified as non-urgent (brake noise, stopping normally). Two tool calls plus end_call. Booked Tuesday, October 6 at 8:00 AM with Mike Alvarez (Quick Service).
